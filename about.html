About

Built for families who need clarity, not more confusion.

MaiCareNow helps families organize care concerns, understand options, and make more informed next-step decisions.

Why MaiCareNow exists

When something changes with a parent, spouse, or loved one, families often have to make important decisions quickly. The information is scattered, the choices are confusing, and the pressure is real.

MaiCareNow brings structure to that moment. We start with the situation, identify what matters most, and help families think through practical options.
