Frequently Asked Questions

Is MaiCareNow a doctor or medical provider?

No. MaiCareNow helps organize information, identify questions, and guide families toward resources. It does not replace medical advice from licensed professionals.

What happens after I complete the assessment?

Your information helps create a practical roadmap with possible next steps, resources, cost-saving opportunities, and questions to ask providers.

Is my information private?

Your information should be handled with care and respect. We only ask for information needed to understand your situation.
