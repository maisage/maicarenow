Frequently Asked Questions

Simple answers to the questions families are most likely to ask first.

What is MaiCareNow?

MaiCareNow is a non-medical healthcare navigation service. It helps individuals and families organize what has changed, understand available options, compare useful resources, and decide what to do next.

Is MaiCareNow a healthcare provider?

No. MaiCareNow does not diagnose, treat, prescribe medication, provide nursing care, or replace a physician or other licensed professional.

What is the free assessment?

It is a structured starting point that asks what changed, what concerns you most, how urgent the situation feels, and what kind of help would be useful. It is not a medical assessment or diagnosis.

What happens after I submit the form?

MaiCareNow reviews the information and contacts you using the method you selected. The next step may be a brief conversation, clarification of your priorities, or an explanation of a useful service or resource.

Does the first step cost anything?

The initial assessment is free. Any paid service, report, scope, and price will be explained before you decide whether to proceed.

Does MaiCareNow receive commissions for recommendations?

No. MaiCareNow does not accept commissions for recommending healthcare providers, care facilities, pharmacies, or other care services.

What should I do in an emergency?

Do not wait for a response from MaiCareNow. Call 911 or contact the appropriate emergency service.

How is my information handled?

MaiCareNow uses submitted information to understand and respond to your request. Review the Privacy Policy and avoid submitting highly sensitive identifiers through the website.
