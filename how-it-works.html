How It Works

A simple process that helps families organize the situation and move forward with more confidence.

1. Share what changed

Tell us what happened, what you are worried about, and what you need help understanding.

2. We organize the options

MaiCareNow looks at medications, assistance programs, care support, benefits, and next steps.

3. Receive your report

You receive practical guidance with clear questions, resources, and suggested next steps.
