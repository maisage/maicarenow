How it works

Simple intake. Clearer next steps.

MaiCareNow is designed to help families organize the situation before making care decisions.

1

Tell us what changed

Complete a short intake form about your loved one, current concerns, care setting, and urgency.

2

We review the full picture

We look across care needs, safety, medications, insurance, support, and family goals.

3

You receive guidance

Your report gives you practical areas to consider and possible next steps to discuss with qualified professionals.

Important: MaiCareNow provides care navigation and decision support. We do not provide medical diagnosis, emergency services, legal advice, insurance sales, or direct clinical care.
