A note from
Mai
Practical guidance. Plain language. No pressure.

I started MaiCareNow because care decisions became real for my own family.

My mom’s care was suddenly placed in my hands while I was still working full time and traveling. Then my mother-in-law’s cancer returned, and her best care option became staying with my husband and me. More recently, our family had to step in again when a loved one could no longer live independently.

Each situation was different, but the feeling was the same: too much information, too many decisions, and not enough clear direction. MaiCareNow helps families slow things down, organize what matters, and figure out the next practical step.

What we help sort out

You may be early in the process, in the middle of a crisis, or just trying to understand what questions to ask.

1

Hospital discharge

Understand what may need to be arranged before your loved one comes home or moves to another care setting.

2

Falls or sudden decline

Sort through what changed, what support may be needed, and what needs attention first.

3

Care options

Compare home care, assisted living, rehab, nursing home care, or family-supported care more clearly.

4

Facility concerns

Review inspection concerns, staffing issues, complaints, ownership patterns, and red flags to look closer at.

5

Medication cost questions

Organize pharmacy, coverage, savings, and provider questions so you know what to ask next.

6

Family decision-making

Turn emotional, scattered concerns into a more practical list of choices, questions, and next steps.

What you get is not a sales pitch.

The purpose is to help you make sense of the situation and prepare better questions before making decisions.

✓ A plain-English summary of the care concern
✓ Questions to ask providers, facilities, agencies, or pharmacies
✓ Options to compare before making a decision
✓ Red flags or missing information to look for
✓ A practical next-step checklist
