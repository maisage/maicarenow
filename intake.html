After you submit the intake

MaiCareNow will review your intake and follow up about a free 30-minute introductory call.

The call is a chance to better understand your situation, talk through what kind of help may be needed, and explain how MaiCareNow can support your next step.

There is no obligation after the introductory call.

If additional support is requested after the introductory call, fees will be discussed before any work begins.
