Then Healthcare Became Personal

Life has a way of changing our priorities. Our family's healthcare journey began when I found myself helping navigate care for my mother. Later, that journey expanded to helping my mother-in-law, then my brother-in-law.

Today, my husband and I continue navigating our own healthcare journey while supporting the people we love.

Each experience brought new questions:

  • Which doctor or specialist should we call?
  • Which medication is right, and what does it cost?
  • Will insurance cover it?
  • Is there a lower-cost option?
  • What should we do next?

I realized families are not short on information. They are overwhelmed by it.

Bringing Technology and Compassion Together

As someone who has spent decades organizing complex information, I kept asking myself one question:

Why isn't healthcare easier to navigate?

That question became the beginning of MaiCareNow.

MaiCareNow is not here to replace doctors, nurses, or caregivers. It is here to help families organize information, understand their options, ask better questions, discover potential savings, and move forward with greater confidence.
