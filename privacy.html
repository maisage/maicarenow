Privacy Notice

Last updated: May 2026

MaiCareNow respects the privacy of individuals and families who contact us for care-navigation support. This notice explains what information may be collected through this website and how it may be used.

Information we collect

When you submit an intake form or contact MaiCareNow, we may collect information such as:

  • Your name and contact information
  • Information you choose to share about a care situation
  • General details about the type of help being requested
  • Any follow-up information you provide by email, phone, or form submission

How we use information

Information submitted to MaiCareNow is used to understand your request, respond to your inquiry, schedule or discuss an introductory call, and provide care-navigation support when appropriate.

How information is shared

MaiCareNow does not sell your personal information. Information may be shared only as needed to operate the website, process form submissions, communicate with you, or if required by law.

Form submissions

MaiCareNow uses a third-party form service to receive intake submissions. Please avoid submitting emergency requests or highly sensitive information through the website form.

Email and communication

If you contact MaiCareNow by email or submit an intake form, we may use your contact information to respond to your message and follow up about your request.

No emergency services

MaiCareNow does not provide emergency services. If there is an immediate safety, medical, or emergency concern, please contact emergency services or the appropriate licensed professional directly.

Your choices

You may contact MaiCareNow to ask questions about your submitted information or request that we delete information you previously provided, subject to any recordkeeping or legal requirements that may apply.

Contact

Questions about this privacy notice may be sent to:
mai@maicarenow.com

This privacy notice is intended to provide a simple overview of MaiCareNow’s current practices. It is not legal advice. As MaiCareNow grows, this notice may be updated.
