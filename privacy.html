Privacy Policy

Effective date: July 25, 2026

MaiCareNow provides non-medical healthcare navigation and educational guidance. This policy explains how information submitted through the website and intake form may be handled.

Information we may collect

  • Contact information, such as your name, email address, telephone number, and preferred contact method.
  • Information you voluntarily provide about care needs, medications, living arrangements, health concerns, recent changes, or desired outcomes.
  • Messages, questions, feedback, and basic website-use information if analytics are enabled.

Do not submit Social Security numbers, financial account information, insurance identification numbers, medical record numbers, passwords, or other highly sensitive identifiers through this website.

How information may be used

  • To review and respond to your request.
  • To prepare for an introductory conversation or assessment.
  • To provide educational information, navigation support, resource comparisons, or a care report.
  • To maintain appropriate records, improve services, protect the website, and comply with law.

MaiCareNow does not sell personal information or use health-related information for targeted advertising.

Intake form and service providers

The intake form is provided through Tally. Information submitted through it is processed through Tally’s systems and made available to MaiCareNow. Other providers may support hosting, email, communications, document storage, and security.

Health-information notice

MaiCareNow is not representing that this website or the current intake form is designed for transmitting protected health information under HIPAA. Submit only the minimum information needed to describe the situation.

Your choices

You may ask MaiCareNow to access, correct, or delete information you submitted, subject to applicable legal and operational recordkeeping requirements. Use the Contact page to make a request.

Emergency notice

Do not use this website or intake form for an emergency. Call 911 or contact an appropriate emergency service.
