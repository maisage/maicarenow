Privacy Policy

Your story may include personal and health-related details. This policy explains what MaiCareNow collects, why we collect it, and the choices available to you.

Important first

MaiCareNow provides non-medical guidance and healthcare navigation support. It is not a healthcare provider, health plan, emergency service, or substitute for professional medical advice.

Do not use this website or the intake form for an emergency. Call 911 or contact an appropriate emergency service.

1. Information we may collect

Depending on how you use the website, we may collect:

  • Contact information, such as your name, email address, telephone number, and preferred contact method.
  • Information about your relationship to the person needing support.
  • Information you choose to share about care needs, living arrangements, medications, health concerns, recent changes, or desired outcomes.
  • Messages, questions, feedback, and other information you submit voluntarily.
  • Basic technical information generated when you visit the website, such as browser type, device type, referring page, and general usage information, if analytics are enabled.

Please share only the information reasonably necessary for MaiCareNow to understand your request. Do not submit Social Security numbers, financial account information, insurance identification numbers, medical record numbers, passwords, or other highly sensitive identifiers through the website.

2. How we use information

We may use submitted information to:

  • Review your request and respond to you.
  • Prepare for an introductory conversation or assessment.
  • Provide educational information, navigation support, care options, resource comparisons, or a care report.
  • Maintain records of communications and services requested.
  • Improve the website, intake experience, and MaiCareNow services.
  • Protect the website, prevent misuse, and comply with applicable legal obligations.

MaiCareNow does not sell personal information. We do not use personal health-related information for targeted advertising.

3. Intake form and service providers

The MaiCareNow intake form is provided through Tally. When you submit the form, information is processed through Tally’s systems and made available to MaiCareNow. Tally states that form data is encrypted in transit and at rest and stored in Europe.

You can review Tally’s privacy notice and data-protection information before submitting the form.

We may also use service providers for website hosting, email, document storage, communications, security, and technical support. These providers may process information only as needed to perform services for MaiCareNow or as required by law.

4. Health-information notice

Information you submit may be sensitive. MaiCareNow is not representing that this website or the current intake form is designed for the transmission of protected health information under HIPAA.

Submit only the minimum information needed to describe the situation. Detailed medical records, clinical documents, and highly sensitive identifiers should be shared only through a method that MaiCareNow has specifically approved for that purpose.

5. When information may be shared

We may share information:

  • With service providers that support the website or MaiCareNow’s operations.
  • With professionals, organizations, or resources you ask MaiCareNow to contact or include in your navigation plan.
  • When reasonably necessary to protect a person from serious harm, protect MaiCareNow’s rights, investigate misuse, or comply with law, legal process, or a lawful government request.
  • As part of a business reorganization, sale, merger, or transfer, subject to appropriate privacy protections.

MaiCareNow will not contact a healthcare provider, family member, facility, or other third party about your situation without your direction or another lawful basis.

6. Retention and security

We retain information only for as long as reasonably necessary to respond to your request, provide services, maintain appropriate business records, resolve disputes, and meet legal obligations.

We use reasonable administrative and technical safeguards appropriate to the nature of the information. No website, email service, form platform, or storage system can guarantee absolute security.

7. Your choices

You may ask MaiCareNow to provide access to, correct, or delete personal information that you submitted, subject to legal and operational recordkeeping requirements. You may also ask us to stop contacting you.

Use the Contact page to make a privacy request. We may need to verify your identity before completing the request.

8. Information about another person

When you provide information about another adult, you are responsible for having appropriate permission or another lawful reason to share it. Avoid submitting more information about another person than is necessary.

9. Children

The website is intended for adults. MaiCareNow does not knowingly solicit personal information directly from children under 13. A parent, guardian, or authorized adult should contact MaiCareNow when seeking guidance involving a child.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the website, services, or legal requirements change. The effective date below will be revised when material changes are made.
