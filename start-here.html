What Happens Next

1

Complete the assessment

Answer simple questions so MaiCareNow can understand what kind of help may be needed.

2

Your answers help create a clearer picture of care needs, medication concerns, cost questions, and family priorities.

3

4

Optional follow-up support

You may choose a follow-up conversation to walk through your options and decide what to do next.
